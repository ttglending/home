Financing Built for Real Estate Investors

DSCR Loans

Qualify on the property's cash flow — not your personal income. Ideal for full-time investors and portfolio builders, from single-family rentals to large multifamily.

  • As low as 15% down (85% LTV)
  • 620 FICO minimum
  • SFR · 2–4 unit · 5–50+ units
  • From $50K · DSCR 1.0 min
  • 30-year fixed available

Conventional Investment

Conventional conforming loans for single-family rentals and 2–4 unit investment properties — the gateway product for investors building their first portfolio.

  • SFR (1 unit) & 2–4 unit properties
  • As low as 15% down (SFR)
  • 620 FICO minimum
  • 15 or 30-year fixed
  • Cash-out refinance available

Portfolio & Blanket Loans

Finance multiple properties — SFR rentals, duplexes, or apartment buildings — under one streamlined loan. Simplify your balance sheet and unlock equity.

  • Cross-collateralization available

  • Release provisions available
